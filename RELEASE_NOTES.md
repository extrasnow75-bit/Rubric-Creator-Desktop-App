## What's new in v1.1.0

### Choose your Canvas course from a list

Instead of finding a course address and pasting it, click **Choose from my courses** on the Target
Canvas Course card. The app lists the courses where you are a teacher, TA or designer, grouped by
term with the newest first. Master and template courses are at the top, and courses that have ended
are labelled. Click one and its address is filled in for you.

The list is on all three screens that ask for a course: the main rubric flow, the screenshot tool and
the upload for existing draft rubrics. Pasting a course address still works, below the list.

Each course shows your role in it. A custom role your school has built on Teacher, Designer or TA,
such as **Course Administrator**, shows under its own name, and those courses are included.

The first time you list courses for a Canvas site, the app asks you to confirm that site before it
sends your Canvas token there. Your token stays in your computer's keychain and is never shown on
screen.

### Fewer Google sign-ins

The Google sign-in now stays valid for longer, so you are no longer asked to sign in again every
week. Google shows an "unverified app" notice the first time you sign in; choose **Advanced**, then
continue. A privacy policy and terms of service are now linked from the sign-in screen.

### Smaller fixes

The **X** button on the Google Drive file window, and on other pop-up windows, could not be clicked
when the window sat under the blue title bar. It now works.
